# Contacts

## Escalation

- L1: returns desk staff and the returns desk lead, in working hours.
  They answer shoppers and send drafts. The desk lead may pull the
  kill switch.
- L2: platform on-call, at any hour. Pages, rollback, kill switch.
- L3: the FDE, until the end of hypercare in week 4. Code questions
  only; L2 makes every change.

## After hypercare

From week 5, L3 is the IT operations lead. The FDE answers questions
by email for one more month and makes no changes.
