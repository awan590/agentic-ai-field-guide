# Value hypothesis: Example Supply returns desk
(synthetic numbers)

## Problem
The desk spends 96.2 hours a week on 640 tickets. 453 a week are
refund status, return label, and policy question tickets: look-ups and
replies that a draft could speed up.

## Baseline
- Tickets per week: 640
- Mean handle minutes: 9.0
- Error rate: 8.6%
- Candidate tickets per week: 453
- Candidate hours per week: 52.5
Source: ch17/tickets.csv, 2 weeks, read by ch17/baseline.py.

## Hypothesis
If the agent drafts replies to refund status, return label, and policy
question tickets, and a person sends each one, handle time on those
tickets falls by 2.0 minutes or more. That frees about 15.1 desk hours
a week.

## Measure
Handle minutes and errors per ticket from the ticket log, pilot
against control in the same two weeks.

## Stop if
- The pilot saves under 1.0 minute per candidate ticket.
- The error rate rises above 8.6%.
- The agent pays a refund with no approval.
